TransportGlyphs.ttf — source icons and licenses
================================================

This bundled icon font contains four transport-mode glyphs the Material Icons
set has no symbol for. Each was normalised from a freely-licensed source icon by
assets/fonts/build_transport_glyphs.py.

  horse     (U+E800)  Material Design Icons "horse-human"
                      https://pictogrammers.com/library/mdi/icon/horse-human/
                      License: Apache License 2.0
                      (c) Pictogrammers / Michael Irigoyen

  gondola   (U+E801)  Temaki "gondola_lift"
  chairlift (U+E802)  Temaki "chairlift"
  tbar      (U+E803)  Temaki "t_bar_lift"
                      https://github.com/rapideditor/temaki
                      License: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)

Material Design Icons is licensed under Apache-2.0; a copy of that license is at
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0 . No attribution is required for the
CC0 (Temaki) glyphs, but their provenance is recorded here for completeness.
